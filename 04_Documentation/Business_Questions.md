# Business Questions

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyse employee attrition patterns, understand workforce characteristics, and identify factors associated with employees leaving the organisation.

The analysis uses Python for data inspection, quality assessment and preparation, followed by Power BI for interactive reporting and visualisation.

## 2. Key Business Questions

The project aims to answer the following questions:

1. **Overall Attrition:** What proportion of employees have left the organisation, and how many employees remain active?

2. **Department and Job Role:** Which departments and job roles have higher employee attrition rates?

3. **Age and Experience:** How does attrition vary across different age groups and levels of work experience?

4. **Workforce Characteristics:** How are factors such as gender, marital status and business travel associated with employee attrition?

5. **Overtime and Compensation:** Is attrition higher among employees who work overtime or belong to lower monthly income bands?

6. **Job Satisfaction and Work-Life Balance:** How does attrition vary across job satisfaction and work-life balance levels?

7. **Tenure and Career Progression:** How does attrition vary with years at the company, years in the current role and time since the last promotion?

8. **Retention Patterns:** What workforce patterns could help HR identify areas where employee retention may need further investigation?

## 3. Scope of Analysis

The analysis focuses on:

- Workforce size and attrition rate.
- Department-wise and job-role-wise attrition.
- Employee demographics and characteristics.
- Overtime and monthly income patterns.
- Job satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Employee tenure, experience and promotion history.

## 4. Expected Business Value

The analysis aims to help HR stakeholders:

- Understand which employee groups have higher attrition rates.
- Identify workforce patterns that may require further investigation.
- Review areas such as overtime, compensation and employee experience.
- Support evidence-based workforce planning and retention discussions.

## 5. Analytical Limitation

The dataset represents a single snapshot of employee information. It does not provide sufficient employment timelines or exit dates for true time-based retention or employee survival analysis.

The findings identify associations and patterns, not proven causes of employee attrition.
